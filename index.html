Travel essentials

Visa, money, SIM, packing, weather

🛂

Visa & entry

30-day visa-free for most passports. Register free at etravel.gov.ph ≤72h before arrival. Keep return flight + hotel proof.

💱

SIM / Wi-Fi

Packing checklist (0/8)

Weather & vaccines

Now: El Nido 30°C, calm seas (dry season Nov–May). Typhoon risk Jun–Oct — keep buffer day.

Routine vaccines + Hep A/Typhoid advised. No mandatory shots for Palawan. Travel insurance with dive cover recommended.

Safety & emergency

One-tap help

Call 911

Hospitals

El Nido Community Hospital • Coron District Hospital • Ospital ng Palawan (PPS, tertiary).

Hospitals map →

Embassies

Most embassies in Manila (3–4h away by air). Save your embassy hotline before island days.

Common scams

Beach-tout “private boats” without license • inflated trike fares • fake “closed lagoon, pay extra” — verify at accredited desks.

Sea safety DOT says: vest on

Life vest on every open-water ride. Skip tours in habagat warnings — coast guard hotline: . Never touch corals or turtles.

Sustainable traveler

Reef-safe sunscreen only, bring-your-own-bottle (refill stations in town), take trash back from islands, keep eco-fee receipts.

Local tips

Language, etiquette & laws

Tipping: 5–10% in restaurants, ₱20–50 for guides. Dress: cover shoulders in churches; swimwear only at beaches. Laws: no littering (fines), no drones without permit in protected areas, no touching corals/turtles.

Do / Don't

Cultural quick list

  • Do: greet with a smile, remove shoes in some homes, ask before photographing rituals.
  • Don't: loud music at Kayangan Lake (sacred), haggle aggressively, feed wildlife.
